# The reference implementation and the tools it is compared with

- Methods: the standard decoder (libjpeg-turbo's, as Pillow calls it); unround with its defaults (TGV), its 8-bit samples as its command line writes them (dithered); jpeg2png with its defaults (50 iterations) and with 500; jpegqs with its defaults (quality 3) and with quality 6; Knusperli.
- Measures, against the original: the PSNR and SSIM (Gaussian weights of 1.5) of the 8-bit samples, and their PSNR-B in grey; the means are over the files. Against the file's intervals: the share of the coefficients of a file encoding the picture that lie within them.
- Files: 1512 test files. A cell of fewer files than the row's says how many it is of: jpeg2png reads no greyscale file, nor a 4:2:0 file of an odd size.
